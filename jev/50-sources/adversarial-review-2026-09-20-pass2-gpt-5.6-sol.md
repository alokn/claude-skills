## D. Verdict

**Fit to serve as ground truth: with caveats.**

The corpus is now much safer than the first-pass version: rollout guidance, deployment eligibility, SDE handling, arithmetic, cross-references, option-limit framing, and the confidence/probability distinction are materially repaired. Its use-case entries are unusually concrete and generally honest about code boundaries and missing measurements.

It is not yet safe as an unqualified source of final verdicts. The largest remaining risk is metadata-level contradiction: an assistant retrieving the FAQ or index will answer `conditional` where the detailed entry says `no as asked`. The remaining calibration/abstention residue and questionable RRF `strong` rating can also recreate exactly the overclaiming the first pass identified.

Top five remaining changes:

1. Align frontmatter, FAQ, body verdicts, and index with the **as-asked** verdict; split rewrites into separate entries where needed.
2. Remove every remaining “far better abstention,” model-as-abstainer, and unqualified calibrated-confidence claim.
3. Delete the SDE-cascade copy-paste from model-difficulty routing and perform a contradiction scan after bulk fixes.
4. Downgrade or re-evidence RRF fusion, and make every `strong` entry’s qualifying labelled measurement a first-class frontmatter source.
5. Rebuild the changelog/index counts, remove invented monthly volumes, and add an explicit failure-mode or non-model-veto statement to every entry.

No files were modified.
