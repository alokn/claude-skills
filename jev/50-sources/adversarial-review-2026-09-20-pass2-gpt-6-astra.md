**D. Ground-truth verdict and top five changes**

**No, in its current form.** It is useful research material, but an assistant can still retrieve contradictory operational instructions, mismatched benchmark claims, or unfair categorical exclusions. The problem is not missing caveats somewhere else; it is incorrect assertions surviving in independently retrievable entries.

1. **Repair evidence-to-design matching:** especially phishing decomposition and RRF primitives, batching and costs.
2. **Propagate corrections into every dependent entry:** remove the false SDE fail-safe, calibration guarantees, abstention attribution and zero-latency claims.
3. **Make verdicts internally consistent:** align question, body, frontmatter, FAQ and index; separate rewritten proposals.
4. **Correct newly sampled design errors:** clause-absence coverage, compaction evidence, commitlint semantics and navigation/backtracking assumptions.
5. **Validate retrieval semantics, not just IDs:** exclude historical reviews, retain metric/label/model provenance, and test adversarial questions against the complete retrieved answer.
